A Story-Poem

The Fire Saint

A tale of creation, the Unknown, the Almighty, and a little chaotic Saint who wandered through existence.
✦
Scroll into the Unknown
✦

Before the stars.
Before the skies.
Before the sea.
Before existence itself had a name.

There was something that could not be explained.

I — Before Everything

The Wonders of Creation

The stars, the skies, the sea, and the shores, Infinite universe and its countless galaxies. The time, the space, the concept of the end; Nowhere to be found, nowhere to be seen.

Existence emerge, reality prevails. The wonders of creation, wonders of wonders. Arguments of creation that start with a bang. Creation of narratives that start with an ink. And a blind chaos that dreamed existence into being.

All are but a speculation inside a written book.

✦

Before existence, before reality, An Actuality that ceased impossibilities. A void, a paradox, absolute nothingness. Nothing is still a thing, a thing exists. The absence of absolute nothing, the absence of silence. None can define, all tongues failed.

The Actuality, The Unknown..

UNKNOWN
II — The Creator

The One Who Created

Emerge the Creator, Creator of all that creates. In the Unknown that all tongues failed. A lonely, a loving Creator. Created the being, the humble chaos.

Oqhoi fȳr — "the mysterious flame" in the tongue that predates all.

The embodiment of heat that annihilates chaos. An inborn flame of his own — Simte Flame. A reckoning force wrapped in humility. The Protector of all that exists.

THE FIRE SAINT
A little barefooted man of 5'5".
Long white hair with blue, calm eyes.
Wrapped in a grey robe adorned with white flames.

The Fire Saint..

The loving, the Almighty Creator, Seeing a being just like himself, A joyous laughter fills the Unknown.

A fragment of creation shaped as a lotus pendant. A gift for the little Saint..

III — Creation

Creator Of Creators

The Almighty Creator, Creator Of All. The joyful Creator, Creator Of Creators. Brought existence into being with mere words.

Creation emerge, existence emerge, reality into being. In the Unknown that swallows paradox, The Almighty is happy..

Existence came into being.

IV — The Unknown Returns

The Things Beyond Existence

The Unknown from the Unknown. Formed from the Unknown. The Unknown that sensed creation. Emerge as the Unknown.

The Unknown seeks to consume Existence as a whole.

But The Fire Saint declined. No entry for the Unknown. Lest the Simte Flame shall answer the Saint's call.

✦

For what seemed endless, though no time existed, The little Saint battled against the Unknowns.

The Unknowns are many, But with Simte Flame that burns the Unknown, Easy work for the little Saint..

Again and again, beyond all measures, The Saint protects, existence prevails.

THE SIMTE FLAME

The flame that burns the Unknown.

The flame that answers the Saint's call.

The flame born from chaos.

The flame that annihilates chaos.

V — The Little Saint

Inside Existence

The Saint is bored from all those battles. He engulfs existence with the Simte Flame.

The Unknowns seek entry, But the flame permits none.

The Saint's journey begins inside existence..

✦

The little Saint, the humble chaos, Journeys through the cosmos in awe.

The little Saint saw the universe, the galaxy, and beyond. Never has he seen something so beautiful. He is in awe.

For the first time ever, the Saint is curious..

VI — The Journey

Eons of Peace

For eons and eons inside existence, The Saint found peace.

His love for playing with nebulae and black holes never ceased.

What a pleasant day it was for the Saint to swim around the Milky Way, Constantly burping up neutron stars..

✦

One occasion, a lucky encounter.

The Saint saw beings that looked a lot like himself, Beings that traveled upon the Earth.

He saw them, understood them. He spent the time of his life around them.

He loved reading their books, Especially those about the belief of beginnings.

But one thing he did not understand..

Why did they decay?
Why did they wither away?

The Saint gazed upon existence, The wonders, the beings, the stories of beginnings.

Yet the question of their fading remained unanswered. He carried the question with him And departed existence, Beyond reality, beyond all creation, Returning once more to the Unknown..

VII — The Duel

The Absolute And The Chaos

The cheeky little Saint, the unbearable Saint, Speaks to the Creator, the Almighty.

He proposes a duel. Not out of malice, Not out of hatred, But a thing to remember forever.

The Creator smiles. The Saint smiles.

The duel begins Between the Absolute And the Chaos..

✦

The little Saint, the humble chaos, Struck the Creator with the Simte Flame.

The flame capable of erasing that which does not exist, An Unknown that swallows paradox, Rendered meaningless beneath the flame.

The Saint's eyes, the eyes of truth, The eyes that judge, That saw, That perceived all,

Failed hard in the face of the Creator, The Creator Of All Creators.

The Saint, the Protector, The worthy flame, Struck the Creator again.

VIII — Heat

Beyond Flame

The Saint channeled all his Simte Flame into one point.

The Simte beam, Born from annihilated chaos within the Unknown, Pierced through the impossible.

How hot can it be?

The Unknown was erased. Half of the Unknown. Again, And again, And again..

But the Creator Did not even bear the scent of fire..

HEAT OVERLOAD

The Saint channels all his capabilities, An utmost impossibility poured into the Simte Flame.

He engulfed himself, Became the pure chaos-annihilating Simte Flame.

No form.
Pure Simte Flame.

Heat beyond heat.
Heat beyond flame.
Heat beyond erasure.

The Saint overloaded the Heat.

All the Unknown And all its Unknowns Vanished.

No more.
Permanent.

A complete Heat That told the Unknown It should be emptier, More than what it already was.

Now there was no more Unknown, Only an emptier nothingness.

IX — The Hem

For The First Time..

But for the first time..

The hem of the Creator's robe
Burned?

The Saint smiled.

And vanished away, Erasing himself for which he could not turn back..

X — Beyond Creation

The Saint Wakes

The chaotic Saint, the humble chaos, Woke up in a space of darkness.

He looked around And saw an arrogant girl With a pen that shapes existence as her desire.

He looked again And saw a chaotic idiot Who dreamed existence into being.

The chaotic little Saint looked further And saw someone who transcends infinitely.

The Saint understood.

📖

He Was In A Book.

A narrative shaped by the very beings that decay and wither away.

A narrative shaped by the very beings That decay and wither away, As he once knew.

He knows he is in a book.

XI — The End?

Until Then..

But for the last time..

The chaotic Saint pointed upward and shouted—

"Well, meet again, you lovely Creator!
Until then... byee!"

And the Saint smiled..

✦

THANK YOU FOR READING MY SILLY STORY

The story ends here.
Or perhaps it never really began.

THE FIRE SAINT
